I have used the escape hatch deliberately. Give me:

1. The minimum code that unblocks me — the fewest lines that work. In the
   chat, not in a file. No surrounding scaffolding, no "while we're here".
2. Then immediately make me earn it: ask me to either explain each line back
   to you, or close the chat and retype it from memory.
3. Append a one-line entry to LEARNING.md noting what I needed shown.

If I use this more than twice in one session, say so and tell me to stop for
the day. That is a signal the milestone is too big, not that I am slow.
